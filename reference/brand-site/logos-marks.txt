Logos and Marks
The NMSU logo is a powerful symbol that serves as the anchor of our brand. The logo set-ups, athletics marks, University seal, and other stationary and mascot marks are also key parts of the system.
 [img ../images/OFFICAL-standard.png alt=''] 
Official NMSU Logo
As the university’s official identifier, the NMSU logo (or institutional mark) is the common thread throughout all university digital and print communications that establishes credibility immediately.
 [img ../images/SETUP-standard.png alt=''] 
Logo Setups
Logo setups create a clear connection between the official NMSU logo, our tagline and your department, college or program name.  
 [img ../images/ATHLETIC-standard.png alt=''] 
Athletics Marks
The NMSU Department of Athletics uses its own set of protected primary, secondary and cultural marks. 
 [img ../images/SEAL-standard.png alt=''] 
University Seal
The seal is restricted for use by and for the Board of Regents and the Office of the President, with limited exceptions.
 [img ../images/CLASSIC-AGGIE-standard.png alt=''] 
Classic Aggie
The mascot mark is a historical symbol that has restricted use due to a licensing agreement with Oklahoma State University since 2014.
 [img ../images/STATIONARY-standard.png alt=''] 
Stationery Mark
The stationery mark can only be used on official NMSU letterhead, envelopes and business cards. You can request a digital letterhead template here.
