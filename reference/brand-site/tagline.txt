Applying the Brand
NMSU Tagline
Our tagline quickly communicates to all audiences our proud tradition of advancing academic, personal and professional success.
 [img ../images/TAGLINE.png alt=''] 
As of late 2021, the tagline is a federally registered trademark and as such always should carry the ® designation. The words "Be Bold" are capitalized and use Gotham Black which highlights the emphasis of taking bold action. The words "Shape the Future" use Gotham Medium. 
You can download a standard NMSU logo setup, which includes the trademarked tagline. If you would like to a customized unit setup for your department, you can contact NMSU Marketing and Communications 575-646-3221 or mktgserv@nmsu.edu.
Restrictions
The NMSU logo can appear without the tagline, but the tagline should never appear without the NMSU logo.
The tagline must include the registered trademark symbol ®.
Do not use different fonts, font weights, and change or remove the punctuation marks.
Do/Don't 
 [img ../images/TAGLINE-do-standard.png alt='Be Bold. Shape the Future Tagline'] 
Do use Gotham for the tagline.
 [img ../images/TAGLINE-dont-fonts.png alt='Tagline with different fonts and punctuation '] 
Don’t use the tagline in any typeface other than Gotham or change the punctuation marks.
 [img ../images/TAGLINE-do-standard.png alt='Be Bold. Shape the Future Tagline'] 
Do make sure the tagline stays on one line.
 [img ../images/TAGLINE-dont-shapes.png alt='Tagline used inappropriately. '] 
Don’t create new configurations or add stylistic treatments, such as placing the tagline on a curve.
