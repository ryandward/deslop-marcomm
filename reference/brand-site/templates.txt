Marketing & Communications
Applying the Brand
Templates
Leveraging the power of the NMSU brand is easy – even if you don’t have the knowledge or background to create eye-catching and compelling materials. We provide the tools and resources you need to reach you marketing and promotional goals.
We offer report templates in Microsoft Word and presentation templates in PowerPoint.
Printed letterhead, business cards, and envelopes that use our official University designs can be ordered at Del Valle Design & Imaging.
We also offer licenses for Marq, a web-based branded template solution. 
MS Office Report and Presentation Templates
Microsoft Word report templates are available with three design options for report cover pages.
Presentation templates are available in standard and widescreen format. Each PowerPoint template contains three different presentation title slides and two divider slides to choose from.
For best user experience, download the template and open it on your desktop.

Create office communications with Marq, a web-based, desktop publishing software. Marq allows NMSU departments to quickly create professional materials that follow NMSU brand standards. Through Marq, you design your own files, get them approved by Marketing and Communications, and then download the files for printing.
The easy-to-use software is accessible through web browsers — no need to download or install anything. This system also provides the campus community direct access to NMSU designers.
We recommend this software to people who frequently create materials such as postcards, posters, flyers and small brochures for their colleges or departments. 
Licenses for Marq are on a first-come, first-served basis. To request a license or for more information, email templates@nmsu.edu.
